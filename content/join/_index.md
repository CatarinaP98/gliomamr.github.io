<p>
GliMR brings together researchers, clinicians, physicists, data scientists, radiographers, students, industry partners and others with an interest in glioma and brain tumour MRI. By joining GliMR, you become part of an international community working together to advance research, education, harmonisation and clinical translation in brain tumour imaging.
</p>
